Stratego — Board & Piece Set

Light parchment theme, Napoleonic field-officer insignia. Red and blue differ on three independent channels — silhouette (chamfered octagon vs. rounded square), surface pattern (vertical pinstripe vs. 45° twill) and emblem (lozenge vs. roundel) — so the armies stay separable without hue. Every token is one element: gradient face, corner insignia mirrored at 180° so it reads from either side of the table, rank numeral centred.
