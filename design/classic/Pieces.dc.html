The figures are original silhouettes drawn on a 24 × 32 grid for this project — hats and attributes tell the ranks apart (bicorne and plume for the Marshal, shako for field officers, kepi for company officers, bearskin for the Sergeant, helmet and pick for the Miner, spyglass for the Scout, brim and collar for the Spy). They evoke the original game without copying its artwork.
